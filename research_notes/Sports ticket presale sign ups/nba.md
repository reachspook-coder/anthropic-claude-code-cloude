Research date: 2026-10-04. Team pages were accessed on that date. Where the page exposed a "last modified" stamp, it is given. Method note: this session's web-search quota ran out partway through. ## 1. How the Spurs and Knicks presales worked (2025-26 season, 2026 playoffs, 2026 Finals)

### Takeaway
The two teams used different mechanisms, and both are still open for 2026-27:
- **Spurs:** the free, app-based **Official Spurs Fan Club** got a presale window right after season-ticket members. For the 2026 first round that was noon on April 1, about 22 hours before the 10 a.m. April 2 public on-sale. Sales were limited to buyers within 150 miles and 4 tickets per order.
- **Knicks:** the free but ID-verified **Knicks Fan First** program emails a *unique* code on presale day. It is the main non-season-ticket route into MSG playoff games.
  - 2026 playoff phases: season ticket members → Fan First + Chase cardholders → general public.
  - For the Finals there was **no general public sale at MSG**.
- **Value:** Finals resale get-in prices at MSG ran about $4,000–$10,000+, against a Chase-presale face-value range of about $2,000–$6,000.

### Inferences
- The buyer's "valuable codes" most likely came from these two channels:
  - **Spurs:** the Spurs Fan Club presale (after season ticket members, before the public).
  - **Knicks:** Fan First invitations, which are unique and ID-tied, not a leaked generic code.
- For the Knicks Finals, Fan First and Chase were effectively the only public routes, since there was no general sale. That explains the size of the premium: Chase-presale face values of about $2,000–$6,000 against get-in resale of about $4,000–$10,000+ is roughly 1.5–5x. This is a rough calculation from different dates and sections.
- Spurs access is restricted by geography (150-mile radius) for playoffs. A buyer outside that radius could not legitimately buy Spurs playoff tickets in 2026, even with Fan Club access. The rule applied to "customers residing" in the radius; how residency was checked was not described.
- Both teams' lists stay "live" year-round. Fan First page edits on Sept. 16, 2026 and the Spurs' Aug. 2025 presale show they are reused for regular-season presales, not only playoffs.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for the exact Spurs **West finals or Finals** sale tiers and dates. The pattern above is documented for round 1 only. The round 2 article mentions only the public sale.
- Not confirmed whether the Spurs Fan Club presale code is shared (one code for all members) or unique/account-linked.
- Not found: the exact dates and times of the Knicks' Fan First playoff presales by round, and whether Fan First requires New York-area residency. The page says eligibility is at "MSG Sports' discretion."
- The 2026-27 Spurs single-game on-sale date and Fan Club presale were not found.
- Not found: whether Fan First had a presale in the Sept. 2026 single-game sale.

### Inferences
- **Registration priority** for a buyer chasing deep-run presales, based only on what is verified:
  1. Knicks Fan First (unique codes; it worked for the Finals).
  2. Spurs Fan Club (but note the 150-mile playoff radius).
  3. 76ers Sixthman (but note the Greater-Philadelphia billing-address rule).
  4. Thunder Insider, Celtics Club Green, Nuggets Insider, Cavs Email Alerts/Cavs ID, Timberwolves emails, Raptors Insider. Playoff value for these is unverified.
- For the Nuggets and Pistons, the documented playoff priority belongs to plan holders, so a free list may add little.
- Several contenders restrict playoff sales by residency (Spurs, 76ers). A non-local US buyer may be legitimately shut out regardless of codes. Check each team's terms per round.
- Seat counts:
  - Knicks Fan First: one invitation per person and 4 tickets per game, so expect small allocations.
  - Spurs and Fan First: 4 per order (Spurs) and 4 per game (Fan First).

## 6. Team policies that restrict resale or transfer

### Takeaway
The documented NBA anti-resale tools are:
- **Delayed transferability:** Knicks playoff tickets locked until 24 hours before the game (72 hours for season ticket holders).
- **ID-verified, non-transferable presale codes:** Knicks Fan First.
- **Residency restrictions with order cancellation:** 76ers (Greater Philadelphia, by billing address); Spurs (150-mile radius).
- **Per-order or per-game caps:** 4 tickets.
- **Non-resellable premium inventory:** Lakers Courtside Reserve.
- **Mobile-only delivery:** Thunder.

No Warriors-specific policy was verified.

### Cited Findings
- **Knicks:**
  - Individual buyers' playoff tickets were not transferable until 24 hours before each home game; season ticket holders' until 72 hours before. MSG: "we're shortening the ticket transfer window for Knicks playoff games." — [NY Post via Yahoo, Apr. 3, 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/knicks-taking-steps-playoff-tickets-173000851.html)
  - Fan First: ID.me verification; "Pre-sale codes may not be purchased, transferred or sold"; one invitation per person; maximum 4 tickets per game. — [Knicks Fan First](https://www.nba.com/knicks/tickets/fan-first)
- **Spurs:** playoff sales limited to residents within 150 miles; maximum 4 per order. — [KSAT, Apr. 1, 2026](https://www.ksat.com/news/local/2026/04/01/how-and-where-to-buy-san-antonio-spurs-home-playoff-tickets/)
