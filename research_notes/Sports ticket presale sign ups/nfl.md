# NFL: Presale Codes, Lotteries, Waitlists and Priority Access (all 32 teams and league events), as of 2026-10-04

Method note for the report writer: Team ticket pages were fetched directly from the 32 official club sites on 2026-10-04 (all on the NFL club-site platform). Sign-up URLs below are the exact links found on those pages. The session's web-search quota ran out partway through, so later facts come from direct fetches of official pages, team sitemaps and news archives, Wikipedia, and a few press articles. Anything not seen on a fetched page is marked "unverified" or listed under Gaps. "STM" means season ticket member, "PSL/SBL" means personal or stadium-builder seat license, and "TM" means Ticketmaster.

## Q1. Top Super Bowl LXI contenders (odds as of 2026-10-03) and how each sold home playoff tickets last postseason (scope update)

### Takeaway
The ten favorites are Bills, Rams, Ravens, 49ers, Seahawks, Chiefs, Bengals, Eagles, Lions and Broncos, with the Jaguars tied at +2000 and the Bears at +2200. NFL playoff priority belongs mostly to paying account holders: season-ticket/PSL holders opt in during December, and paid deposit waitlists at some teams (Bears, Patriots, Vikings, Bills, Bengals) add presale rights. Free sign-ups mostly give you alerts. Even so, several teams put "potential home playoff game" tickets on public sale through Ticketmaster in November or December, refunding them if the game isn't hosted: Chiefs on Nov 13, 2024, Rams on Dec 11, 2024, Bears in December 2025, Jaguars on Dec 10, 2025. Being logged in and alerted for those on-sales is the realistic free route.

### Inferences
- From these precedents, contenders will likely open STM opt-ins and "potential home game" public on-sales between mid-November and mid-December 2026 (Chiefs Nov 13, Rams Dec 10–11, Jaguars Dec 10, Bears Dec 11, Ravens opt-in deadline Dec 9). The practical playbook is to have a logged-in Ticketmaster or SeatGeek team account and to turn on team ticket alerts and app notifications by early November.
- The Chiefs' Jackson County presale is the only documented NFL residency-gated playoff presale found. A non-resident can't use it.
- The Super Bowl route through teams is account-based: PSL/STM lotteries (Ravens, Rams precedent) or On Location team packages released right after the conference championship (Seahawks precedent, so around Jan 31, 2027 for LXI). It isn't a shared code.

## Q2. Rundown: the NFL's big ticketed events and how the public gets in (2026–28)

### Takeaway
Regular-season and playoff games sell through each team's primary platform: Ticketmaster for most teams, SeatGeek for the Ravens, Commanders, Cowboys and Saints, with partial SeatGeek use by the Cardinals and Titans. Season-ticket holders always go first. The NFL doesn't sell Super Bowl tickets to the general public; the public routes are On Location packages, sweepstakes, an ADA drawing that is closed for LXI, and resale. The Draft is free through the NFL OnePass app. International games need an NFL Ticketmaster account and sold out or went on sale in June–July. The Pro Bowl Games are reported discontinued.

### Cited Findings
- **Playoffs (Wild Card Jan 16–18, Divisional Jan 23–24, 2027)**: STMs/PSL holders opt in first, followed by team public on-sales of "potential" home games with refunds if not hosted (Chiefs, Rams, Bears, Jaguars examples in Q1) — [Chiefs, Nov 11, 2024](https://www.chiefs.com/news/tickets-for-potential-postseason-games-at-geha-field-at-arrowhead-stadium-set-to-go-on-sale-wednesday); [Rams, Dec 9, 2024](https://www.therams.com/news/rams-2024-playoff-ticket-information); [Bears, Dec 11, 2025](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chicago-bears-announce-ticketing-information-for-potential-home-postseason-games-2025); [Jaguars FAQ](https://www.jaguars.com/playoffs/faqs). Primary face-value playoff tickets are sold through Ticketmaster and "many playoff games sell out quickly"; SeatGeek-primary teams such as the Ravens sell on SeatGeek instead ([Ravens single game](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/tickets/single-game)) — [TicketIQ playoff guide (2026 edition; page byline still shows Jan 22, 2024)](https://blog.ticketiq.com/blog/nfl-playoff-tickets).
- **Conference Championships (Jan 31, 2027)**: Team news archives show a recurring headline pattern of "limited number of tickets for AFC Championship Game ... to go on sale Monday" (Chiefs, Patriots); only the URLs were seen, not article text — [Chiefs sitemap entry](https://www.chiefs.com/news/limited-number-of-tickets-for-afc-championship-game-to-go-on-sale-monday); [Patriots sitemap entry](https://www.patriots.com/news/limited-number-of-patriots-afc-divisional-playoff-game-tickets-to-go-on-s-285926). The 49ers listed a public Ticketmaster event for a potential home NFC Championship — [49ers postseason](https://www.49ers.com/postseason/).
- **Super Bowl LXI (Feb 14, 2027, SoFi)**: Official packages are sold by On Location; see Q4 for prices — [On Location SB LXI](https://onlocationexp.com/nfl/super-bowl-tickets). Participating teams run STM/PSL lotteries — [Ravens playoffs page](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/tickets/playoffs/). The NFL ADA random drawing runs Feb 1–June 1 of the year before the game — [SF Standard, Jan 27, 2023](https://sfstandard.com/2023/01/27/how-to-get-super-bowl-tickets-tips-tricks-for-scoring-passes-to-the-big-game/). Resale goes through the NFL Ticket Exchange (Ticketmaster) and other marketplaces — [Chiefs tickets page](https://www.chiefs.com/tickets/).
- **NFL Draft 2027 (Apr 29 – May 1, 2027, Washington, D.C.)**: Free. NFL OnePass is "required to participate in any games or activations on site." On Location sells VIP packages — [NFL.com draft dates](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-announces-dates-for-2027-draft-in-washington-d-c); [NFL OnePass](https://www.nfl.com/apps/one-pass/); [On Location Draft](https://onlocationexp.com/nfl/nfl-draft-tickets).
- **International games**: European games require an NFL Ticketmaster account (https://www.nfl.com/international/ticketaccount), with a limit of six tickets per account per game — [NFL International ticket account](https://www.nfl.com/international/ticketaccount); [NFL Paris page](https://www.nfl.com/international/games/paris/).
- **Pro Bowl Games**: Reported discontinued; the NFL announced it in August 2026 according to Wikipedia, which cites TSN, "NFL pulls the plug on the Pro Bowl" — [Wikipedia, 2026 Pro Bowl Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pro_Bowl_Games).

### Inferences
- For a US buyer chasing playoff and Super Bowl access, the main levers are: (1) logged-in team accounts plus alerts for the Nov–Dec "potential game" public on-sales; (2) paid waitlist or priority-list deposits at teams that grant presales (Bears, Patriots, Vikings, Bills, Bengals); (3) On Location's refundable Super Bowl Priority Access deposit for LXII and later; (4) free sweepstakes for LXI (U.S. Bank, open until Dec 31, 2026).

### Gaps
- The Pro Bowl discontinuation rests on a single secondary citation (TSN via Wikipedia); no NFL.com release was fetched.

## Q3. Team-by-team presale and priority sign-ups (all 32 teams)

### Takeaway
No NFL team was found sending shared presale codes to free email registrants. Free sign-ups (ticket alerts, interest forms, SMS) are notification-only. Real priority comes through accounts: season-ticket/PSL membership, or paid waitlist deposits at the Bears ($100/seat, non-refundable, includes an individual-game presale), Patriots ($100/seat, refundable, special presales and subject-to-availability playoff access), Vikings ($100/seat, refundable), Lions ($100/seat, non-refundable, ID-verified), Bengals ($150/seat, non-refundable) and the Bills (seat deposit, amount not public). Free waitlists exist at the Broncos (Mane List, over 100,000 names) and Commanders (new-stadium waitlist). The Eagles' waitlist cost isn't stated. Packers sign-ups are by mail, plus a Brown County residents-only drawing.

### Inferences
- **Shared vs. unique codes**: none of the 32 team pages advertised a shared presale code for free registrants. NFL presales identified are tied to an account (STM, PSL or waitlist account in Ticketmaster or SeatGeek), a payment card (Jackson County billing ZIP), or a lottery (STM/PSL Super Bowl drawings, U.S. Bank sweepstakes). Free alerts and register-interest lists notify you of public on-sales but don't grant early access. This differs from the NBA-style shared-code experience the buyer had with the Spurs and Knicks.
