# MLB and NHL: presale codes, priority access, and marquee-event registrations (status as of 2026-10-04)

Method note for the report writer. Most findings come from official club and league pages that I fetched directly on 2026-10-04: mlb.com team pages, nhl.com team and event pages, and the Formstack sign-up forms embedded in them. For those I give the URL and write "accessed 2026-10-04". Dated news articles carry their publication dates. Some items came only from a search-engine result summary because the page itself blocked fetching, and I mark those "(search-result summary; page not fetched)". The session's web-search budget ran out partway through, so a few news-only questions are listed as gaps.

## 1. TIME-SENSITIVE: the 2026 MLB postseason and World Series. Who is still alive, how each club sells postseason tickets, and what is open right now

### Takeaway
Eight clubs are alive in the Division Series (Oct 3–10): Rays, Guardians, Yankees and White Sox in the AL; Brewers, Dodgers, Braves and Padres in the NL. The World Series runs Oct 23–31, 2026. None of these eight clubs' postseason pages (checked Oct 4) shows a random-drawing registration. They sell first to plan holders, then to member, resident or "Insider" presale tiers, then to the public. For a US buyer outside Wisconsin and the San Diego region, these are open now:
- The Rays already have World Series tickets on public sale.
- The Guardians have a "World Series interest" form open.
- The Padres will run a separate World Series presale for their geo-limited "Padres Insiders".
- The Brewers will sell World Series seats "at a later date".
- The Yankees, Dodgers and Braves had posted no World Series method as of Oct 4.

Several eliminated clubs ran the MLB-standard random drawings that wanted this kind of buyer: Red Sox, Astros, Phillies and Cubs. Their windows have closed and their programs are moot for 2026, but they show how next year's drawings will likely work.

## 2. "The White Sox got into something": what it is, and how White Sox postseason ticket access works

### Takeaway
The White Sox made the 2026 MLB postseason. It is their first trip since 2021, coming after three straight 100-loss seasons. They clinched on Sept 24, swept the Astros in the AL Wild Card Series, and now face the Guardians in the ALDS. There was no random-drawing registration:
- ALDS home tickets on Ticketmaster are sold out.
- ALCS and World Series access is being sold by tying it to buying a 2027 Full Season Ticket Plan.
- The free "White Sox Insider" list promises "presale opportunities, including postseason access."
- Resale for the Rate Field ALDS games runs about $159 to $1,000+.

## 3. MLB club-level presale/Insider sign-ups, ticketing platforms and postseason practices (all 30 clubs)

### Takeaway
Every MLB club has a free "Insider" email/newsletter page at a uniform URL, mlb.com/{club}/fans/newsletters. Both that URL and mlb.com/{club}/forms/newsletter-registration returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-04 for all clubs, except that the Angels and Phillies "forms" URLs return 404. An MLB.com account is required for postseason drawings. Primary ticketing splits three ways:
- 22 clubs are verified on MLB's own Tickets.com / MLB Ballpark app.
- Seven use Ticketmaster: Yankees, Braves, White Sox, D-backs, Mariners, Rockies and Blue Jays.
- The Padres sell through padres.com/mytickets plus the Ballpark app; the underlying platform is unverified.

Many clubs explicitly promise "presale" access to Insiders or text subscribers. Postseason access, though, depends on the club's own drawing or tier rules each fall.

## 7. Resale premiums, resale restrictions, and shared versus account-tied access (both leagues)

### Takeaway
MLB's official fan-to-fan resale partner is SeatGeek. Club postseason terms commonly do one or more of the following:
- prohibit resale where law or MLB policy forbids it;
- cancel tickets bought for resale (Braves);
- bar resale entirely for presale purchases (Giants member presale, Royals drawing tickets);
- tie drawing wins to an MLB.com account and a matching billing address.

On the NHL side, the Capitals point fans to NHL Ticket Exchange (Ticketmaster) as the only guaranteed resale, and the Rangers use ID-verified unique codes. Premiums are large: about 3–4x regular season for White Sox ALDS games, and $650–$1,600 get-in for the 2025 World Series.

### Inferences
- Shared "Insider" codes (where clubs use them) can leak. The highest-value MLB and NHL routes found here are account-, address-, ID- or geo-tied, so the codes themselves have little transfer value. Value comes from being registered under one's own verified account, one registration per person, as the rules require.
- Several clubs explicitly cancel resale-intended purchases or ban resale on presale-acquired seats (Braves, Giants, Royals). Presale access should be treated as for personal use.

### Gaps
- I found no league-wide MLB or NHL resale price cap or face-value-only resale policy, beyond the club-level terms above.
- I have no 2026 NHL playoff or Stanley Cup Final resale price data.
