# Presale & priority ticket sign-ups: WNBA, MLS, NWSL, PWHL and newer leagues (Unrivaled, PLL, LOVB/MLV), as of Oct 4, 2026

Research notes prepared 2026-10-04. Notes on method and confidence:
- "Verified" means I fetched the official team or league page on 2026-10-04 and the sign-up link or terms appeared there. "Unverified" means the detail came only from a search-result excerpt, from a page that would not render or returned a 403/404 error, or from a secondary outlet.
- This session's web-search budget ran out partway through the work (a session-wide limit of 200 searches). After that I could only fetch pages already identified. As a result, several WNBA teams (Sky, Lynx, Mercury, Storm, Mystics, Sparks, Wings) and several MLS clubs were not researched. See the Gaps sections.
- In this set of leagues, nearly every priority program is a paid, time-stamped deposit or waitlist tied to one ticketing account, not a shared presale code. Where a generic code or link exists, it is flagged.

## 1. WNBA: league status, team presale and waitlist programs, expansion deposits, All-Star 2027, Finals

### Takeaway
The WNBA has labor peace through 2032. A 7-year CBA was ratified in March 2026, so a work stoppage does not threaten 2027 tickets. 2026 set attendance records, and the 2026 Finals begin Oct 17. For the highest-demand teams, the real priority access comes from paid, account-tied waitlist deposits: Valkyries $25/seat (100-mile residency rule), Aces $25/seat (AXS), Fever priority waitlist, Liberty premium waitlist (which also gives Nets/Barclays presales), Dream $100/seat (Georgia/200-mile rule), Tempo CAD $100/$250, Fire (Portland DMA only). Cleveland (2028) takes a $28 deposit now. Detroit (2029) and Philadelphia (2030) offer only free "notify me" forms. The 2027 All-Star Game is at Chase Center on July 30–31, 2027, and its ticket process has not been announced.

### Inferences
- The CBA runs through 2032, so 2027–2028 season tickets and deposits carry no labor-stoppage risk. That was not true in 2025, when the CBA had been opted out.
- For a US buyer who is not local, the most accessible high-value lists are:
  - Aces ($25/seat on AXS; no residency rule found)
  - Fever priority waitlist (no residency rule shown)
  - Liberty premium waitlist (non-refundable; its extra benefit is presales for Nets and Barclays Center events, which resembles the user's Knicks experience)
  - Cleveland's $28 SeatGeek deposit (no residency rule found)
- Residency rules exclude non-locals from the Valkyries (100 miles), Dream (Georgia or 200 miles), and Fire (Portland DMA) lists.
- Every program found is an account-tied, time-stamped priority position, not a shared code. Earlier deposits get better seat-selection order, so early sign-up matters most for Cleveland (2028). It will matter for Detroit and Philadelphia once their deposits open.
- 2027 All-Star tickets will probably favor Valkyries and Warriors season members and waitlist holders, since host teams usually get priority. This is speculation: nothing has been announced. Watch valkyries.wnba.com and wnba.com in spring 2027.
- Finals tickets are sold only by the two finalists, after the series are set, with members and waitlists first. A non-member's realistic path is the public on-sale or resale. Resale can collapse when the opponent or matchup is uncertain, as the Fever example shows (−90%).

## 2. MLS: team presale and waitlist programs, MLS Cup 2026, Leagues Cup 2027, MLS All-Star 2027, platforms

### Takeaway
MLS is switching calendars, so three MLS Cups fall inside the window: Dec 18, 2026 (hosted by the better regular-season team), May 2027 (end of the 14-game "Sprint" season, Feb–May 2027), and May 2028 (end of the first summer-to-spring season, which starts July 2027). MLS Cup tickets are sold by the host club only days before the match, with season-ticket members first. In 2025 Inter Miami sold to members on Monday Dec 1 and to the public Tuesday Dec 2 at 10 a.m. ET, for a Saturday Dec 6 final, with a 6-ticket limit. The best club-level priority options now are 2027 deposits and season memberships: Inter Miami (new stadium; deposit holders went first), LAFC, NYCFC's $10 refundable Etihad Park deposit ("The 718" waitlist; stadium opens July 17, 2027), Sounders, Atlanta United, and San Diego FC. Leagues Cup 2027 and MLS All-Star 2027 details have not been announced.

### Cited Findings

**Calendar and MLS Cup 2026**
- MLS Cup 2026 is on Friday, Dec 18, 2026, hosted by the team with the better regular-season record. It is the last December final.
  - Wild-card matches: Nov 18
  - Round One (best-of-3): Nov 20–Dec 2
  - Conference semifinals: Dec 5–6
  - Conference finals: Dec 11–12
  - Source: [Field Level Media, Mar 16, 2026](https://fieldlevelmedia.com/mls/mls-cup-2026-final-scheduled-for-dec-18/); also [MLS announcement](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/major-league-soccer-announces-audi-2026-mls-cup-playoffs-schedule)

### Inferences
- The free forms (Detroit, Philadelphia, KC interest) only give notice of when deposits open. The queue position is set by the deposit timestamp after that.
- The paid deposits ($10–$100 per seat; CAD $100–$250 for the Tempo) buy ordered priority rather than a code. Refundability varies:
  - Refundable or convertible: NYCFC (refundable), Aces (convertible after 2 years), PWHL (convertible).
  - Non-refundable: Valkyries, Tempo, Liberty.
- No source mentions lottery or ballot systems, Verified Fan, or ID-linked tickets for these leagues. Exceptions to standard tickets are digital-only ticketing (PWHL San Jose; Denver Summit at Centennial Stadium, where screenshots are not accepted).
