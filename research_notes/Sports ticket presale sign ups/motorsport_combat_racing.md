## 1. Formula 1: US races (COTA, Las Vegas, Miami), Canada and Mexico, F1's own programs, new Americas races, Monaco and Silverstone

### Takeaway
F1 does not run a free newsletter code like UFC or WWE. US-race priority comes from **(a) deposits/priority lists run by each promoter** (LVGP deposit window; COTA 2027 deposits open now), **(b) American Express card-gated presales**, **(c) official package deposits (F1 Experiences)**, and **(d) F1's official ticket-store waitlists**, which are currently open for Austin, Las Vegas, Mexico and Canada 2027. ## 3. UFC: newsletter code mechanics, Fight Club, Fight Pass, venue presales, the White House event, notable cards

### Takeaway
UFC's standard sequence, confirmed for UFC 334 at MSG, is **Fight Club presale on Wednesday → UFC Newsletter presale on Thursday → public on-sale on Friday at 10 a.m. ET**, roughly 7 weeks before fight night. Venue and sponsor presales (e.g., MSG's "Polymarket UFC 334" sign-up) run alongside. Fight Club gives each paying member a **unique code** (max 6 tickets per code). The newsletter code is effectively a shared list code. The White House card (UFC Freedom 250, June 14, 2026) sold no tickets: South Lawn seats were invitation-only, and 85,000 free Ellipse fan-fest tickets went out by registration.

### Inferences
- The newsletter presale is free and lands 24 hours before the public on-sale. Because one code goes to the whole list and third-party sites republish UFC codes, it should be treated as a **shared/generic** code. Fight Club is **unique and account-tied** (one code per member, 6 tickets) and gets 48 hours of lead time. That is the main reason to pay for it on high-demand PPVs.
