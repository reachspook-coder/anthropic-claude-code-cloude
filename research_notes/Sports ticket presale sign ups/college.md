# College sports ticket lotteries, presales and priority access (US): research notes as of 2026-10-04

Scope: sign-ups that are open now or open between now and 2028 for NCAA Final Fours and early rounds, the College Football Playoff (CFP) and its bowls, and other marquee college events, plus how donor priority works. "Shared code" means the same promo code goes to everyone and often gets posted publicly. "Account-tied" means access is bound to one application, account, donor record or ID. Method note: the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Most later facts come from direct fetches of official pages. Items marked **snippet-only** come from search-result summaries whose page I could not open, so treat them as unverified.

## 1. NCAA Men's and Women's Final Four: lottery mechanics, 2027/2028 sites and windows, early-round tickets, official exchange

### Takeaway
Both Final Fours now sell public tickets mainly through a paid, randomized lottery tied to one account. The fee is $25 and non-refundable. Applicants also prepay the full $400 per ticket, can ask for up to 4, and get upper-level seats only. The lottery opens in spring of the year before the event and closes May 31. The 2027 windows (men's in Detroit, women's in Columbus, the women's first-ever lottery) closed May 31, 2026, and men's results are due in fall 2026 (one aggregator says by Oct 31). The 2028 lotteries (men's in Las Vegas, women's in Indianapolis) have not been announced. If the last two cycles hold, they should run from about April to May 31, 2027. Early-round and regional tickets are sold through the NCAA's Ticketmaster Account Manager. Their presales come from free NCAA email sign-ups plus shared promo codes: "LASTSHOT" started Sept 30, 2026, and "MARCH25" was the 2024 regional code.

## 2. College Football Playoff 2026-27: title game, first-round campus games, quarterfinal/semifinal bowls, format

### Takeaway
2026-27 is the third season of the 12-team CFP. The title game is Mon Jan 25, 2027 at Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas. I found no public lottery or Verified Fan registration for it. Most tickets go to the two finalists, who sell them by donor priority; each school got 20,000 in January 2026. Ticketmaster is the official resale marketplace (snippet-only) and Playoff Premium handles hospitality. Five of the six quarterfinal and semifinal bowls have already gone on sale: Fiesta on Aug 24 (SeatGeek), Sugar on Sept 1, Rose on Sept 2 (max 4), plus Orange and Peach (all four on Ticketmaster). The Cotton Bowl says it will go on sale "at a later date" on SeatGeek. Bowl "priority" comes from loyalty programs, not free code lists: Sugar annual-ticket deposits, Orange Champions Club, and the Fiesta Legacy Membership earned by buying two years in a row. First-round campus games are sold by the host school in order: season-ticket holders, then donors, then the public. Visiting schools get about 3,500 tickets.

## 4. Donor/booster priority points and alumni association access

### Takeaway
At major programs, postseason access follows donor "priority points" (or a similar ranking), frozen at a cutoff date. Season-ticket holders and donors get invitations in rank order. For the scarcest inventory, such as road playoff games and title games, only top-ranked donors receive offers. Demand far exceeds allocations: Indiana had 31,000 requests for 20,000 title-game seats in January 2026. Alumni association membership alone does not appear to grant postseason presale rights. Ohio State's alumni ticket process requires a gift and covers regular-season home games.

### Cited Findings
- **Texas A&M (12th Man Foundation), 2025 season** — [12th Man postseason timeline](https://app.12thman.com/2025STHPostseasonTimeline):
  - Priority for all postseason requests was locked by priority-point rank as of Thu Nov 13 at 5 pm CT.
  - SEC Championship requests: emails Nov 6, deadline Nov 13.
  - Home CFP first round: season-ticket requests by Dec 3, then a donor presale Dec 11 and the public sale Dec 12.
  - Road first round: only "a limited number of donors — based on priority point rank as of Nov. 13," about 3,500 tickets in total.
  - Quarterfinals, semifinals and title game: all eligible donors and season-ticket holders emailed Dec 7, deadline Dec 15.
  - Allocation is by priority ranking, not lottery.
- **Oklahoma (Sooner Club), Dec 2025:**
  - Season-ticket holders' access times were set by "Sooner Club membership level and Championship Points associated with the account" — [SI, Dec 7 2025](https://www.si.com/college/oklahoma/football/how-can-oklahoma-fans-get-tickets-for-the-college-football-playoff-01kbx1eepdsf).
  - Request details for the quarterfinals, semifinals and title game went to season-ticket holders Dec 10 — same source.
  - The public allotment sold out the same day it opened — [News9](https://www.news9.com/sports/university-of-oklahoma/cfp-tickets-in-norman-ou-releases-sale-times-for-alabama-playoff-game).
- **Army (Army A Club), 2026 Army-Navy game:** giving tiers set both presale order and limits. AD's Circle ($25,000+) bought first, April 7, with up to 30 tickets. Ranger ($100–199) bought last, June 17, with 2 tickets — [ArmyGameday](https://www.armygameday.com/army-navy-tickets).

  - Fiesta Legacy Membership: automatic after two consecutive years of purchases — [Fiesta](https://www.fiestasportsfoundation.org/sports/2025/2/11/fiesta-legacy-membership.aspx).

### Inferences
- Joining a booster club now probably won't help for this season's playoff. Texas A&M's 2025 cutoff was mid-November, and points build with years of giving. Joining before the 2027 season could help with future conference title games and road playoff games, but top-tier access for title games and road playoff games goes to large, long-term donors.
- Alumni association dues by themselves do not look like a presale route for bowls, the CFP or the Final Four. Where alumni processes exist, they are tied to annual giving and to regular-season home games.
- Donor priority is account-tied; nothing is shared. Applying from multiple accounts is barred by household or account limits (e.g., the Sugar Bowl's one account per household).
