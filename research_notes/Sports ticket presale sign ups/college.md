# College sports ticket lotteries, presales and priority access (US): research notes as of 2026-10-04

Scope and priority: per the buyer, the NCAA Men's and Women's Final Four come first (Section 1, the most detailed), the College Football Playoff second (Section 2), and other events are brief (Section 3).

Terms used below:
- **Shared code:** the same promo code goes to everyone and is often posted publicly.
- **Account-tied:** access is bound to one application, account, donor record or ID.
- **snippet-only:** the fact came from a search-result summary whose page I could not open. Treat it as unverified.

How the research was done: the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Later facts come from direct fetches of official pages (raw HTML via curl where the page needs JavaScript), noted as "fetched 2026-10-04."

## 1. NCAA Men's and Women's Final Four and March Madness: lottery mechanics, other public routes (presale and alert lists, official exchange, hospitality), and 2027–2028 sites and dates

### Takeaway
The public's main face-value route into both Final Fours is now an NCAA lottery tied to one Ticketmaster account:
- $25 non-refundable application fee, plus the full ticket price ($400 per ticket covering both semifinals and the final) charged when you apply.
- Up to 4 tickets per application, upper level only, seats assigned at random.
- Reportedly one application per household (snippet-only).
- Runs in spring of the year before the event and closes May 31. Refunds (minus the fee) go to those not selected.

Where each cycle stands:
- **2027:** the men's (Detroit) and women's (Columbus) windows closed May 31, 2026. Columbus was the women's first lottery. Men's results are due "fall 2026" (one aggregator says by Oct 31, 2026).
- **2028:** the men's (Las Vegas, Allegiant Stadium) and women's (Indianapolis, Lucas Oil Stadium) lotteries are not announced. The last two cycles suggest a window of about April to May 31, 2027.

Other official public routes:
1. The NCAA Ticket Exchange, run on Ticketmaster. Resale listings must be at or above face value, and the 2027 Final Fours are not listed there yet.
2. NCAA Fan Experience hospitality by On Location (NCAA.com/VIP), already on sale. Women's Final Four travel packages start at $3,095 per person.
3. Free NCAA sweepstakes.
4. Free NCAA and host-committee alert lists. These feed early-round and regional presales, which use shared promo codes ("LASTSHOT" in Sept 2026, "MARCH25" in Nov 2024).

NCAA ticket terms bar resale above face value except through channels the NCAA approves.

### Inferences
- **Most likely 2028 window:** about early April to May 31, 2027, for both Final Fours. This is inferred from the 2026 lottery (open through May 31, 2025) and the 2027 lottery (open by early April through May 31, 2026). Watch NCAATickets.com, NCAA.com/final-four, the Indianapolis committee newsletter and the Las Vegas host committee from March 2027. - **How the Spurs/Knicks experience translates:** only the early rounds and regionals behave like NBA presales. There, free NCAA lists feed generic codes (LASTSHOT, MARCH25) that circulate publicly. Final Four face-value seats come mainly from the account-tied, prepaid lottery. Registering for lists alone won't get Final Four seats; it gets you the lottery announcement and hospitality or resale notices.
- **Public routes, from cheapest to most expensive:**
  1. Lottery: $400 face plus a $25 fee per entry, with odds unknown.
  2. NCAA sweepstakes: free but long odds.
  3. NCAA Ticket Exchange, with face value as the floor.
  4. On Location hospitality: hundreds to thousands of dollars per person.
  5. Unofficial resale, which NCAA terms discourage and above face value prohibit outside approved channels.
- **Resale tension:** NCAA terms bar above-face resale except in NCAA-approved channels, and the NCAA Exchange explicitly allows listings at or above face. The Exchange therefore appears to be the approved channel. Lottery winners who can't attend should resell there.
- **Bigger 2028 venues:** Allegiant Stadium for the men and Lucas Oil Stadium for the women (13,000 more seats than planned) could mean more lottery inventory in 2028 than in 2027. That is not confirmed.

## 2. College Football Playoff 2026-27: title game, first-round campus games, quarterfinal and semifinal bowls, format

### Takeaway

Most title-game seats go to the two finalists, who sold them to "season-ticket holders, donors, students and others" at face value in January 2026, with 20,000 seats each. Ticketmaster is the official resale marketplace (snippet-only), and Playoff Premium (run by REVELxp) is the verified official hospitality program.

- Fiesta: Aug 24, on SeatGeek.
- Sugar: Sept 1, Ticketmaster.
- Rose: Sept 2, Ticketmaster, max 4.
- Orange and Peach: on Ticketmaster.

## 4. Donor/booster priority points and alumni association access

### Takeaway
Postseason access at major programs runs on donor "priority points" or tiers, frozen at a cutoff date, with offers going out in rank order. The scarcest seats (road playoff games, title games) go only to top-ranked donors. Alumni association membership alone does not appear to grant postseason presales. Ohio State's alumni ticket process requires a gift and covers regular-season home games.

### Cited Findings
- **Texas A&M (12th Man Foundation, 2025)** — [12th Man timeline](https://app.12thman.com/2025STHPostseasonTimeline):
  - Priority rank for all postseason requests was locked as of Nov 13 at 5 pm CT.
  - SEC Championship requests were due Nov 13.
  - Road first-round seats went only to "a limited number of donors — based on priority point rank," about 3,500 in total.
  - Quarterfinal, semifinal and title-game requests went to all eligible donors and season-ticket holders Dec 7, due Dec 15.
  - Allocation is by priority ranking, not lottery.
- **Oklahoma (Dec 2025):** season-ticket holders' access times were set by "Sooner Club membership level and Championship Points" — [SI, Dec 7 2025](https://www.si.com/college/oklahoma/football/how-can-oklahoma-fans-get-tickets-for-the-college-football-playoff-01kbx1eepdsf).
- **Army A Club (2026 Army-Navy):** the giving tier set both presale date and ticket limit. AD's Circle ($25,000+) bought April 7 with up to 30 tickets; Ranger ($100–199) bought June 17 with 2 — [ArmyGameday](https://www.armygameday.com/army-navy-tickets).
- **Indiana (Jan 2026 title game):** 31,000 requests for a 20,000-ticket allocation — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/cfp-national-championship-tickets-prices-miami-indiana-game/).
- **Ohio State Alumni Association** runs an "annual football ticket process" for alumni — [OSU Alumni: Buckeye Football Tickets](https://www.osu.edu/alumni/alumni-perks/buckeye-football-tickets):
  - The 2026 process has concluded.
  - Eligibility for 2027 requires a gift in 2026, received by Dec 31, 2026. Pledges without a payment don't count.
  - The page does not mention bowl or CFP access.

### Inferences
- Joining a booster club now probably won't help for 2026-27 playoff seats, because cutoffs fall in mid-November and points build over years.
- Alumni dues alone are not a presale route for bowls, the CFP or the Final Four.
- Donor priority is account-tied. Household and account caps (e.g., the Sugar Bowl's one account per household) bar applying from multiple accounts.

### Gaps
- No school's dollar-to-points formula was retrieved.
- No information found on how schools distribute Final Four seats when their team makes it.
