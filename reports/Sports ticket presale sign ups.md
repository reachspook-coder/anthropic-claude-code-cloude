# Register early, verify once, catch championship presales

Your Knicks and Spurs windfall came from two of the strongest free programs in American sports, and most leagues have nothing like them. MSG's ID.me-verified **Knicks Fan First** emails each member a unique code. Because there was **no general public sale for the 2026 Finals at MSG**, Fan First and a **Chase** cardholder presale were the only ways in for anyone without season tickets. The free **Spurs Fan Club** got a presale about 22 hours before the public, but only buyers living **within 150 miles** of San Antonio could use it. Free team lists still produce real presale tiers in the NBA, NHL and MLB, such as Rangers Fan First, the Lightning's Insider newsletter and Padres Insiders; the White Sox list promises postseason presales in writing. NFL, MLS and WNBA playoff and championship seats go overwhelmingly to season members and paid deposit holders. Final Four face-value seats come from a prepaid NCAA lottery ($25 fee plus $400 a ticket) that should reopen around April 2027.

This week, act on four things:
- **MLB postseason:** the Rays already have World Series tickets on public sale, the Guardians have a World Series interest form, and the White Sox Insider list promises postseason presales.
- **WNBA Finals:** they start Oct. 17.
- **March Madness early rounds:** a presale is running on the shared code LASTSHOT.
- **NBA Cup championship:** join the interest list. The Dec. 11 final has moved from Las Vegas to **9,100-seat Hinkle Fieldhouse** in Indianapolis.

NFL playoff on-sales come next, from mid-November to mid-December, and MLS Cup follows on Dec. 18. Three cards cover nearly every card presale found: **Chase** (MSG and Chase Center), **American Express** (the NFL's payments partner from 2026) and **Capital One** (NCAA, Orange Bowl).

The hard limit now is eligibility, not finding codes. Residency gates, transfer locks, per-account caps and Ticketmaster's cross-account identity matching all apply, and the FTC is pursuing BOTS Act cases over multi-account buying. Register once per program, in your legal name, and resell only through official exchanges.

**How to read this.** Every table gives the exact sign-up and what it delivered in the most recent cycle. It also gives the type of access:
- **free list**

Two flags mark weaker information. **(Unverified)** means the item was not confirmed on an official page; it came from a search-result snippet, a page that blocked fetching, or a secondary source. **(Projected)** means the date is inferred from last cycle, not announced. Odds are sportsbook snapshots from Oct. 3–4, 2026 and move weekly.

### Set up each platform account once, in your legal name

Ticketmaster tells fans to get "Team Presale" codes by joining team email lists and "Venue Presale" codes from venue newsletters ([Ticketmaster](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/9670692314897-Where-do-I-get-a-presale-or-offer-code)). The platform now polices identity hard:
- **Ticket limits apply across accounts.** Ticketmaster counts accounts "associated with the same name, email address, billing address, credit card number or other information" toward one limit. It can cancel orders and block future purchases without notice ([Ticketmaster](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/9781245025937-Why-is-there-a-ticket-limit)).
- **Persona security checks.** You may need a live selfie and sometimes a government ID. **The account name must match your legal ID**, and failing the check blocks new purchases ([Ticketmaster](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/37371579224209-Why-is-my-Ticketmaster-Account-requiring-a-Security-Check-through-Persona)).
- **No merging.** Accounts cannot be combined ([Ticketmaster](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/26797867792401-Can-I-merge-or-combine-my-Ticketmaster-Accounts)).
- **One login for teams.** The same login works across every team's Account Manager site at am.ticketmaster.com ([Ticketmaster](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/9760688108945-How-do-I-use-my-sports-team-or-venue-account)).

Other programs verify identity through ID.me instead: MSG's Fan First programs and the Sugar Bowl's annual-ticket list ([Sugar Bowl](https://allstatesugarbowl.org/sports/2024/11/22/sign-up-for-access-to-purchase-annual-tickets-for-future-sugar-bowl-games.aspx)).

## NBA: Knicks Fan First still leads, and the Cup final shrinks to 9,100 seats

The 2026 Finals were Knicks vs. Spurs; the Knicks won 4–1, ending June 13 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NBA_playoffs)). The two programs that got you in worked very differently.

**Spurs, 2026 first round.** Sales ran in three tiers ([KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/news/local/2026/04/01/how-and-where-to-buy-san-antonio-spurs-home-playoff-tickets/); [News4SA](https://news4sanantonio.com/sports/spurs-zone/san-antonio-spurs-reveal-playoff-ticket-details-presale-info-texas-frost-bank-center-fans-basketball-wemby-postseason)):
- Season-ticket members: 10 a.m., April 1.
- The free Fan Club: noon, April 1.
- The public: 10 a.m., April 2.

Purchases were **limited to residents within 150 miles of Frost Bank Center**, 4 per order. The team said the radius "prevents scalpers," and the same rules applied to the second-round public sale ([KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/news/local/2026/04/29/spurs-announce-second-round-playoff-ticket-sales-fan-experiences-for-home-games/)). The sale tiers for the West finals and Finals were never published.

**Knicks Fan First.** You register through a form, verify identity with ID.me, and get **a unique code by email on presale day**. Invitations are limited to one per person, purchases to 4 tickets per game, and "pre-sale codes may not be purchased, transferred or sold" ([Knicks](https://www.nba.com/knicks/tickets/fan-first)).
- **2026 playoff order:** season members, then Fan First and Chase cardholders together, then the public. The program had passed **165,000 members**. Individual buyers' playoff tickets **could not be transferred until 24 hours before tip** ([NY Post via Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/knicks-taking-steps-playoff-tickets-173000851.html)).
- **Finals:** there was no general sale at MSG ([PIX11 via Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/knicks-offer-fan-presale-nba-170015777.html)).
- **Growth:** membership has more than tripled since the program passed 50,000 sign-ups in April 2024 ([MSG Sports](https://s23.q4cdn.com/716592820/files/doc_news/FAN-FIRST-PROGRAM-BEST-PLACE-FOR-KNICKS-AND-RANGERS-FANS-TO-GET-PLAYOFF-TICKETS-AT-FACE-VALUE-2024.pdf)). Codes stay unique, but each one competes for a smaller share of seats.

**Where the money was.** The resale spread was concentrated at MSG:
- MSG Game 3: get-in passed $4,000 on StubHub. Vivid Seats' cheapest seat was $4,749 and its average $7,683, against an average of just over $1,000 for the 2025 Finals ([Sporting News via Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/most-expensive-cheapest-nba-finals-182224039.html)).
- San Antonio Games 1–2: get-in was $969 ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/nba-finals-ticket-prices-options-fans-knicks-spurs/story?id=133482833)).

## WNBA and MLS: championship seats go to members, so deposits beat newsletters

In both leagues, playoff and final presales were tied to season-member or deposit accounts in every documented case. Free lists got, at most, a later tier for side events.

There is a counterweight: **prices in these leagues often fall after the first sale**.
- **WNBA:** the Fever's first-round get-in price fell from $308 on Sept. 3 to $28 on Sept. 23 ([Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/caitlin-clark-fever-playoff-tickets-121546507.html)).

Unlike at MSG, waiting is a legitimate strategy here.

Reselling some tickets is legal in most places, but the rules below determine where, how and at what price. They are described here, not worked around.

## Conclusion

The 2026 season shows that the scarce asset has shifted from codes to eligibility. The programs that paid off (Fan First's ID-checked unique codes, the Spurs' radius-limited fan club, MLB's address-matched drawings) were each built to reward one real, verified, often local person. Teams are tightening these programs because playoff tickets became profitable to flip: MSG shortened its transfer window "to disrupt" brokers, and the Spurs pitched their radius as a way to stop scalpers. That changes the strategy from "register with every contender" to two layers. First, register everywhere it's free. Second, expect to actually buy only where you live or where no residency gate exists. In practice that favors MSG (no residency rule found for Fan First), NCAA lists, MLB drawings open to any US resident, and the few deposits with no location test, such as the Aces'. The single cheapest upgrade is a $0-fee Chase card, which shared the Fan First tier at MSG in 2026.

The second shift is that priority is increasingly sold with next season attached. Four of the eight clubs still alive in MLB tie 2026 postseason priority to buying a 2027 plan, and NFL, WNBA and MLS deposits buy a place in line rather than tickets. Paid priority therefore makes sense only where the deposit is refundable or converts into tickets you'd use anyway, as with the Patriots and Aces. The FTC is litigating multi-account buying as a BOTS Act violation, and MSG locks transfers until 24 hours before tip, so any resale plan should assume official exchanges, price caps in a few states, and tax reporting. The 2026 MSG Finals spread came from an unusual combination: no public sale, plus Finals games at MSG, where get-in prices ran about four times San Antonio's. Treat it as a windfall, not a model to plan around.
